`@cosyte/dates` is an independent open-source project. Cosyte is **not affiliated with, endorsed by,
or sponsored by** any standards body or company named in this repository or its documentation.

## Why these names appear

This package works with the dates that parsers for published healthcare data standards return, and
renders them in a published date format. It cannot say which values it accepts, or what it writes,
without naming those standards.

Every reference is **descriptive**: it identifies a standard, and nothing more.
